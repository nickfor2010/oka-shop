Our story

A small dessert, just right

OKA started with a simple idea: a small, well-balanced dessert can change an afternoon.

We bake in small batches in Tainan, layer each cheesecake by hand into a 220g jar, seal it fresh and ship it chilled. There is no storefront, just a kitchen and a lot of jars.

Give your jar a second life

Our jars are light, sturdy plastic, so they travel safely. Please don't throw them away: follow us on Instagram for reels with easy ideas to reuse them.
