隱私權政策 · Privacy

最後更新 Last updated: 2026-11

我們收集的資料

  • LINE 登入時：LINE 使用者 ID、顯示名稱與大頭貼。
  • 下單時：姓名、手機號碼、電子郵件（選填）、收件地址、出貨日、備註，以及匯款帳號後五碼。

用途

僅用於處理與寄送你的訂單、核對匯款，並透過 OKA 官方 LINE 帳號傳送訂單通知。我們不會販售或出租你的資料。地址與電話會提供給物流業者以完成配送。

保存與你的權利

訂單資料依稅務與會計需要保存。你可以隨時透過 LINE @637xgnpi 或 okaintouch@gmail.com 要求查詢、更正或刪除你的個人資料（個人資料保護法第 3 條）。

What we collect

  • When you log in with LINE: your LINE user ID, display name and profile picture.
  • When you order: name, mobile number, email (optional), delivery address, ship date, note, and the last 5 digits of the account you paid from.

How we use it

Only to process and ship your order, match your bank transfer, and send order updates from the OKA LINE Official Account. We never sell or rent your data. Your address and phone number are shared with the courier to deliver your order.

Keeping and your rights

Order records are kept as required for tax and accounting. You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time on LINE @637xgnpi or at okaintouch@gmail.com.
